      Print estimate

        Printer settings

        G-code is written the way EasyGCode writes it: its start and end blocks, each move's E from the bead model (w − h)·h + π·(h/2)² (mm³ on the pellet printer, mm of filament on the P1P), and the part fan from the second layer. The spiral disc and the vase spiral match EasyGCode's own G-code line for line. G-code drives a real machine: look at the toolpath before printing, and watch the first layer.

        About this build

        Projects are recipes, and recipes make G-code and STL files. The Rust engine runs them as WebAssembly in a background worker, three.js draws on the GPU, and the Chat tab's agent (Claude, with your own key) writes and edits recipes with you.
